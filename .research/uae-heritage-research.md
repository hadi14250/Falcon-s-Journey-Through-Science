## 1. Abu Dhabi / أبوظبي

### Quick facts
- **Capital city:** Abu Dhabi city (also the capital of the whole UAE).
- **Famous for:** Largest emirate, seat of government, Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque, huge desert oases.
- **Iconic landmark:** Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque — giant white mosque with 82 domes, fits 40,000+ people.
- **Unique geography:** Liwa Oasis on the edge of the Empty Quarter (Rub' al Khali), the largest sand desert in the world. Tal Moreeb dune ~300m tall.
- **Cultural fact:** "Abu Dhabi" means "Father of the Gazelle." Bedouin hunters from the Bani Yas tribe followed a gazelle to a freshwater spring on the island.

### Learning topics
1. Abu Dhabi is the capital of the UAE and the largest of the seven emirates.
2. The Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque has 82 white marble domes and the world's largest hand-knotted carpet.
3. Sheikh Zayed bin Sultan Al Nahyan was the founding father of the UAE; the mosque is named after him.
4. Liwa Oasis sits on the edge of the Empty Quarter, one of the biggest sand deserts on Earth.
5. The Louvre Abu Dhabi has a giant silver dome that creates a "rain of light" effect inside.

### Landmarks
- Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque — 82 domes, fits 40,000+ worshippers.
- Louvre Abu Dhabi — dome made of 7,850 stars, "rain of light" inside.
- Qasr Al Watan — the Palace of the Nation, a presidential palace with a giant golden dome.
- Yas Island — Ferrari World, Yas Waterworld, Warner Bros. World theme parks.
- Liwa Oasis & Tal Moreeb Dune — green oasis + ~300m dune (almost as tall as the Eiffel Tower).

### Vocabulary
- Mosque / مسجد (masjid) — a place where Muslims gather to pray.
- Oasis / واحة (waaha) — a green spot in the desert with water and palm trees.
- Desert / صحراء (sahraa) — a very dry place with lots of sand and very little rain.
- Capital / عاصمة (aasimah) — the main city of a country, where the leaders work.
- Gazelle / غزال (ghazaal) — a small fast desert animal with curved horns; Abu Dhabi is named after it.

### Did you know?
The carpet inside the Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque is the largest hand-knotted carpet in the world — bigger than a soccer field, weighs 35 tons (as much as 7 elephants), tied by ~1,200 artisans using more than 2 BILLION knots.

### Map node theme
"Grand Mosque silhouette under a desert moon" — white domes/minarets, golden Liwa dunes behind.

## 2. Dubai / دبي

### Quick facts
- **Capital city:** Dubai city.
- **Famous for:** World's tallest building, futuristic skyline, malls, gold and spice souqs.
- **Iconic landmark:** Burj Khalifa — 828m tall, world's tallest building.
- **Unique geography:** Palm Jumeirah, world's largest artificial palm-shaped island.
- **Cultural fact:** Old Dubai was a small fishing/pearl-diving village along Dubai Creek (Khor Dubai). Wooden dhow boats still sail there today.

### Learning topics
1. Dubai is home to the Burj Khalifa, the tallest building in the world at 828 meters.
2. Palm Jumeirah is a man-made island shaped like a palm tree, built using sand from the sea.
3. Dubai Creek (Khor Dubai) was the heart of old Dubai, where people fished and traded by boat.
4. The Dubai Frame is a giant golden picture frame 150m tall — see "old" and "new" Dubai at the same time.
5. Hatta is a mountain area of Dubai where you can kayak on a calm blue dam.

### Landmarks
- Burj Khalifa — 163 floors, observation decks, world's tallest.
- Burj Al Arab — luxury hotel shaped like a dhow's white sail.
- Palm Jumeirah — man-made palm-tree-shaped island.
- Dubai Frame — 150m golden picture frame, world's largest.
- Al Fahidi Historic District — Dubai's oldest neighborhood with wind towers (pre-AC cooling).
- Hatta — mountain town with Hatta Dam kayaking.

### Vocabulary
- Skyscraper / ناطحة سحاب (naatihat sahaab) — a very tall building (literally "sky scratcher").
- Souq / سوق — traditional Arab market for spices, gold, fabric, food.
- Dhow / داو — traditional wooden sailing boat for fishing/trading.
- Creek / خور (khor) — a narrow strip of seawater that flows into the land.
- Pearl / لؤلؤة (lu'lu'ah) — shiny round gem grown inside an oyster.

### Did you know?
The Burj Khalifa is so tall you can watch the sunset from the top, take an elevator down, and watch the same sunset AGAIN from the bottom. More than twice as tall as the Eiffel Tower.

### Map node theme
"Burj Khalifa skyline with a dhow sailing on Dubai Creek" — futuristic spire next to a wooden boat.

## 3. Sharjah / الشارقة

### Quick facts
- **Capital city:** Sharjah city.
- **Famous for:** Cultural and book capital of the Arab world, 20+ museums.
- **Iconic landmark:** Al Noor Mosque — white mosque on Khalid Lagoon, two tall minarets, big central dome.
- **Unique geography:** Only emirate that touches BOTH the Arabian Gulf AND the Gulf of Oman.
- **Cultural fact:** Named "Cultural Capital of the Arab World" by UNESCO in 1998 and UNESCO World Book Capital in 2019.

### Learning topics
1. Sharjah is called the cultural capital of the Arab world because of its many museums and books.
2. Sharjah is the only emirate with land on two seas — the Arabian Gulf and the Gulf of Oman.
3. The Mleiha area is part of a UNESCO World Heritage site where humans lived 100,000+ years ago.
4. Khor Fakkan is a beautiful beach town on the east coast, surrounded by Hajar Mountains and the Gulf of Oman.
5. The Sharjah Museum of Islamic Civilization shows how Muslim scientists invented things like algebra and the modern hospital.

### Landmarks
- Al Noor Mosque — modeled after Turkish architecture, central dome + 2 minarets.
- Sharjah Museum of Islamic Civilization — Muslim science, art, inventions.
- Mleiha Archaeological Centre — 130,000-year-old stone tools, UNESCO World Heritage.
- Al Qasba & Khalid Lagoon — waterfront area, fountains.
- Khor Fakkan — beach town between Hajar Mountains and Gulf of Oman.

### Vocabulary
- Culture / ثقافة (thaqaafah) — art, music, food, traditions of a people.
- Museum / متحف (mathaf) — building showing important historical/art/science objects.
- Heritage / تراث (turaath) — things passed down from ancestors.
- Mountain / جبل (jabal) — a very tall rocky landform.
- Coast / ساحل (saahil) — where the land meets the sea.

### Did you know?
Scientists at Faya in Sharjah found stone tools proving humans lived there ~130,000 years ago — older than the pyramids. In 2025 the Faya Paleolandscape became a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

### Map node theme
"Open book with two minarets and Hajar mountain peaks behind."

## 4. Ajman / عجمان

### Quick facts
- **Capital city:** Ajman city.
- **Famous for:** Smallest emirate, world's biggest dhow shipyard.
- **Iconic landmark:** Ajman Fort & Museum — 18th-century fort with thick coral-stone walls, now a museum.
- **Unique geography:** 16 km of white sandy beach, creek through the city.
- **Cultural fact:** Founded by the Al Nuaimi tribe in the 18th century; rulers still come from that family today.

### Learning topics
1. Ajman is the smallest of the seven emirates of the UAE.
2. Ajman has the world's biggest dhow building yard, where workers still build wooden boats by hand.
3. The Ajman Museum is inside an old fort built in the 1700s to protect the ruler.
4. Al Zorah Nature Reserve has thick mangrove forests where pink flamingos come to feed.
5. Ajman has a 16 km coast of soft white sandy beaches along the Arabian Gulf.

### Landmarks
- Ajman Museum (Ajman Fort) — 18th-century fort with watchtowers, swords, manuscripts, pearl-diver story.
- Ajman Dhow Building Yard — biggest in the world, traditional wooden boats.
- Al Zorah Nature Reserve — mangroves, lagoons, pink flamingos.
- Mowaihat Archaeological Site — 4,000-year-old Bronze Age tomb with pottery and copper tools.
- Ajman Corniche Beach — long sandy beach.

### Vocabulary
- Fort / حصن (hisn) — a strong building with thick walls used to protect people.
- Mangrove / قرم (qurm) — a tree that grows in salty water along coasts, with leg-like roots.
- Flamingo / فلامنغو (flamingo) — tall pink bird that stands on one leg.
- Boat / قارب (qaarib) — small vessel for traveling on water.
- Tribe / قبيلة (qabilah) — group of families sharing the same ancestors.

### Did you know?
Dhow boats built in Ajman are made entirely from wood using techniques over 1,000 years old — without a blueprint! Master builders carry the design in their heads.

### Map node theme
"Wooden dhow on a calm creek with a fort tower behind."

## 5. Umm Al Quwain / أم القيوين

### Quick facts
- **Capital city:** Umm Al Quwain city.
- **Famous for:** Quiet lagoons, mangroves, ancient archaeological sites; one of the calmest, greenest emirates.
- **Iconic landmark:** Umm Al Quwain Fort and Museum — built 1768 to guard the entrance to the old town.
- **Unique geography:** Long narrow peninsula surrounded by lagoons, mangroves, and small islands.
- **Cultural fact:** Name means "Mother of Two Powers" — strong on both land and sea.

### Learning topics
1. Umm Al Quwain means "Mother of Two Powers" because its people were skilled on both land and sea.
2. It is the second smallest and the least crowded emirate in the UAE.
3. Al Sinniyah Island is home to the UAE's biggest colony of Socotra cormorants (15,000+ pairs).
4. The ancient town of Ed-Dur traded with the Roman Empire about 2,000 years ago.
5. People in Umm Al Quwain have always lived from the sea — fishing and pearl diving for centuries.

### Landmarks
- Umm Al Quwain Fort & Museum — coral-stone, built 1768.
- Al Sinniyah Island — Socotra cormorants, Arabian gazelles.
- Ed-Dur Archaeological Site — one of the largest ancient sites on the Arabian Gulf.
- Khor Al Beida Lagoon & Mangroves — kayaking, fish, birds.
- Falaj Al Mualla Oasis — date palm farms inland.

### Vocabulary
- Lagoon / بحيرة (buhayrah) — shallow body of seawater partly closed off from the open sea.
- Pearl diving / غوص اللؤلؤ (ghaws al-lu'lu') — old job of swimming deep underwater to collect pearls.
- Falaj / فلج — ancient water channel from mountains to farms.
- Seabird / طائر بحري (taa'ir bahri) — bird that lives by the sea and eats fish.
- Island / جزيرة (jazirah) — piece of land surrounded by water.

### Did you know?
Umm Al Quwain has been home to people for at least 6,000 years. At Al Sinniyah Island, archaeologists found one of the oldest pearling towns ever discovered in the world.

### Map node theme
"Mangrove lagoon with cormorants flying over a small fort."

## 6. Ras Al Khaimah / رأس الخيمة

### Quick facts
- **Capital city:** Ras Al Khaimah city.
- **Famous for:** Tallest mountain in the UAE (Jebel Jais), world's longest zipline, one of the oldest port cities on Earth.
- **Iconic landmark:** Jebel Jais — highest peak in the UAE at 1,934m.
- **Unique geography:** North of the UAE between Hajar Mountains and the sea, rugged cliffs, occasional snow.
- **Cultural fact:** Name means "Top of the Tent" — named after palm-frond tents (arish) early settlers built between the sea and the creek.

### Learning topics
1. Jebel Jais in Ras Al Khaimah is the highest mountain in the UAE at 1,934m tall.
2. The world's longest zipline is on Jebel Jais — 2.83 km long, speeds up to 150 km/h.
3. Ras Al Khaimah is one of the oldest port cities in the world, with people living there for over 7,000 years.
4. The UAE's pearl diving tradition is celebrated at the Suwaidi Pearl Farm, the only Arabian pearl farm in the world.
5. Dhayah Fort is the only hilltop fort still standing in the UAE; you climb 239 steps to reach the top.

### Landmarks
- Jebel Jais — 1,934m, sometimes snows in winter.
- Jebel Jais Flight — world's longest zipline, ~3 km, up to 150 km/h.
- Dhayah Fort — only hilltop fort left in the UAE; 239 steps.
- Suwaidi Pearl Farm — only Arabian pearl farm in the world.
- Al Marjan Island — man-made island shaped like coral.

### Vocabulary
- Mountain / جبل (jabal) — very tall rocky part of the Earth.
- Zipline / منزلق (munzaliq) — cable you slide down, hanging from a harness.
- Port / ميناء (meena) — place by the sea where boats and ships come.
- Oyster / محار (mahaar) — sea creature with hard shell that can grow a pearl inside.
- Tent / خيمة (khaymah) — simple shelter; "Ras Al Khaimah" means "Top of the Tent."

### Did you know?
It snows in the UAE! On the very top of Jebel Jais, snow has fallen a few times — once in 2004, again in 2009, and a few times since. Roughly once every five years.

### Map node theme
"Mountain peak with a zipline cable and a tiny snowflake."

## 7. Fujairah / الفجيرة

### Quick facts
- **Capital city:** Fujairah city.
- **Famous for:** Only emirate fully on the Gulf of Oman (eastern coast); mountains, beaches, oldest mosque in the UAE.
- **Iconic landmark:** Al Bidya Mosque — oldest known mosque in the UAE, built ~1446 (575+ years ago) using mud and stone.
- **Unique geography:** Most mountainous emirate; Hajar Mountains run right down to the Indian Ocean coastline.
- **Cultural fact:** "Fujairah" comes from an Arabic word meaning "water that gushes up from under the mountains" — fresh springs.

### Learning topics
1. Fujairah is the only emirate that sits entirely on the Gulf of Oman, on the UAE's east coast.
2. Al Bidya Mosque is the oldest mosque in the UAE, built around 1446 using only mud and stone.
3. The Hajar Mountains run through Fujairah, making it the most mountainous emirate.
4. Fujairah gets more rain than any other emirate, so farmers grow mangoes, oranges, and vegetables there.
5. Snoopy Island, off Al Aqah Beach, is famous because it looks like the cartoon dog Snoopy lying on his back.

### Landmarks
- Al Bidya Mosque — oldest mosque in the UAE (~575 years old), four small unequal domes, no wood.
- Fujairah Fort — 16th-century stone fort on a small hill.
- Snoopy Island — looks like Snoopy lying on his back, coral reefs and turtles around it.
- Al Aqah Beach — golden beach, snorkeling, clownfish, sea turtles.
- Hajar Mountains (Fujairah section) — get rain, run with wadis, old villages and forts.

### Vocabulary
- Wadi / وادي — dry valley between mountains that fills with water when it rains.
- Coral reef / شعاب مرجانية (shi'aab marjaaniyyah) — colorful underwater wall made by tiny sea animals.
- Turtle / سلحفاة (sulahfaah) — sea reptile with a hard shell.
- Dome / قبة (qubba) — round roof of a mosque.
- Spring / نبع (nab') — place where fresh water flows up from underground.

### Did you know?
Al Bidya Mosque was built without using a single piece of wood. Its four domes rest on one central pillar of stone and mud — an ingenious design from over 575 years ago. UNESCO is considering it for World Heritage status.

### Map node theme
"Old four-domed mosque at the foot of green mountains, with Snoopy Island in a turquoise sea behind."
